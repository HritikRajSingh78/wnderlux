Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a price quote for my trip? ▾

You can use our Trip Cost Calculator for an instant estimate, or book a free consultation for a fully personalised quote from one of our travel experts.

Do you offer travel insurance? ▾

Yes. We partner with leading travel insurance providers and can include comprehensive coverage in your package. We strongly recommend travel insurance for all international bookings.

Can I modify or cancel my booking? ▾

Absolutely. We understand that plans change. Modification and cancellation policies vary by supplier, but our team will always work to find the most flexible options for you at time of booking.

How far in advance should I book? ▾

For popular destinations and peak seasons (Christmas, school holidays), we recommend booking 3–6 months in advance. For off-peak travel, 6–8 weeks is usually sufficient — though we can often accommodate shorter notice.
